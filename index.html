Fare Iti

Build your own fare and make a life on the island, one level at a time. Each level opens new crafts, plants and animals.

  1. 1Gather and build. Coconuts, fronds, logs and basalt raise your fare: paepae, pou, frame, walls, roof. Then step inside and furnish it.
  2. 2Eat. Bananas, fei from the mountain slopes, fish from the lagoon. Cook at your fire pit.
  3. 3Water and taro. Find the spring, fill coconut cups, dig and flood a trench, plant taro.
  4. 4Weave and make monoʻi. Bamboo strips become mats and fish traps. Tiare flowers and coconut oil become monoʻi.
  5. 5Animals. A dog, a hen, a cat, a pig and a goat live on the motu. Feed them until they trust you.
  6. 6Pension de famille. Later, open a guesthouse: welcome guests, sell your harvest, and buy what you can’t make from the goélette.
  7. 7Crafts and the open sea. Shell necklaces, tapa, pāreu and tīfaifai, music on the vivo and toʻere, and a sail to cross the reef.
